Privacy notice

Puja Dekho · Last updated 11 Nov 2026

Puja Dekho helps you find Durga Puja and Jagadhatri Puja pandals on a map. This notice explains what we keep about you, why, and how to remove it.

When you use Puja Dekho without signing in

  • We count visits to the app once a day per phone, using a small cookie. The count has no name or account in it.
  • We count how many people are using the app right now, with a random code that changes and is deleted within minutes.
  • Your location never leaves your phone. Near me works out distances on your phone only.

When you sign in with Google

We keep:

  • your Google name and profile photo, to show on your profile and on badges you choose to share
  • your Google account ID, so your pujas stay with you on any phone
  • the pujas you mark as visited, the pujas on your wish list, your badges and your crowd reports

We do not keep your email address, your contacts or anything else from your Google account.

Counting the pujas you visit

If you turn on Count visits with location, your phone notices when you have spent 2 minutes at a puja and asks if you are visiting. If you say yes, we save the puja and the time. Your location is not sent. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

Crowd reports

When you report a puja as Free or Crowded, we save the puja, your answer and the time with your account. Other people see only the combined result, such as “Crowded · 4 reports”, never your name. Only reports from the last hour are shown, and every report is deleted after a day. Your phone checks that you are at the puja; your location is not sent.

Who can see your data

Only Puja Dekho. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not show ads. Data is stored with our hosting providers, Vercel and Turso.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything saved with it, write to prasun.vesis@gmail.com from any address and tell us the name on your Google account. We delete it within 7 days.

Children

Puja Dekho is not meant for children under 13, and they should not sign in.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes too.
